Design study · Men ⇄ Youth

Six ways to switch platform.

Every concept lives inside the real management-room chrome and speaks our language — Oswald display, DM Mono labels, paper + ink + a single accent that flips red for Men, warm gold for Youth. No women platform yet; each one leaves room to add a third later. All six are live — click them.

01

Editorial Word Switch

The platform name IS the headline. Tap the ghosted word to bring it forward — a moving underline rules under the active one. Feels like typography, not a toggle.
