BRIT Sport Group · Youth platform · Management room

The youth desk.

Same management room as the men's platform — identical bones: paper and ink, Oswald headlines, DM Mono labels, the black rail, the bordered signal strip, the chipset filters. The one deliberate shift is the accent: where men run on gold, youth runs on a cooler pitch-teal. Everything else that makes youth different is content, not chrome — age-group as the organizing axis, the IFA as the data source, and guardians as first-class contacts.

Difference 01

Teal, not gold

One restrained accent, swapped across every active state, tag and headline word. Reads as the same system in a younger key.

Difference 02

Age group over value

Prospects have no market value. The U-13 → U-21 ladder takes its place — as the signal, the filter, and the tag on every card.

Difference 03

IFA & guardians

Data comes from football.org.il. Season form (apps · goals · assists) and a guardian contact row sit where TM value and agents live for men.
