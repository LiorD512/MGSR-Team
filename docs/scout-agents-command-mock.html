How this maxes out the agents' work

The old screen (and my first mock) treated the agents like a flat bucket of players. But the agents' real output is a daily operation at scale, and that's what should feel alive. Four moves do it:

  • The sweep funnel — "2,418 scanned → 384 matched → 251 approved → 37 sign-now." This single strip turns invisible background work into a visible, impressive daily result. All of it is already logged in ScoutAgentRuns (profilesFound, profilesBeforeReview, profilesRejected, leaguesScanned) + the Director's approvedCount/rejectedCount.
  • "New since your last visit" — the reason to open this every morning. Profiles carry discoveredAt; anything newer than your last session is fresh. This is the hook the old screen completely lacked.
  • The 44-scout roster with grades + pick counts + fresh deltas — makes the network tangible: who's producing, who's recycling. Grades/approval/freshness already exist in ScoutAgentRuns.sportDirector.agentReports. Click a scout → filter the board to its picks (no inner tab, just a filter).
  • Convergence surfaced — "found by 2+ scouts" and "also in rumors" are the Director's highest-conviction signals (per your skill doc) and are computed today but shown nowhere.

No thumbs. Shortlisting is the feedback. No inner tabs — it's one scrolling command surface: sweep → what's new → the calls, with the scout roster as a filter rail. Everything shown is already produced by your pipeline; the lift is the API exposing the funnel + director fields (which it already reads) and persisting the cross-agent list.
