Alpha board / Card design study

Card designs.

Three ways to render the Alpha Board cards, each borrowing from an existing BRIT screen you already like. Same data (hunt metric, match score, why-now, market value). Pick one — or mix.

1

Intelligence Dossier

Release-Alerts DNA · dark flag-background header with portrait, light body with the ranking-metric bar, a 3-stat strip, and actions. Premium & information-rich.

2

Scout Row

Shortlist/Roster DNA · compact horizontal row. Scan many players fast — portrait + name on the left, the hunt metric in the middle, score & value on the right. Highest density.

3

Hero Portrait

Big-visual board · full-bleed portrait with gradient, score ring + verdict overlaid, metric & why-now below. Most striking — fewer per screen, maximum impact.
