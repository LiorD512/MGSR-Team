Does merging work? Here's the honest read.

The unifying model: both screens were always "a board of signable players in your band." They only differed in what drives the ranking — your Director's judgment, or a market metric. So the merge isn't two things glued together; it's one board with a lens switch. The band slider, position chips, cards, dossier drawer and shortlist are shared. Only the top controls + each card's headline change with the lens.

The payoff the two separate screens can never give you — convergence. When a player your Director flagged SIGN also tops the market hunt (the ★ badge + the banner), that's the Edwards-method "multiple independent signals agree" moment your own skill doc calls the highest-conviction call. You literally cannot see that when the data lives in two screens.

Reasons to merge
One mental model ("the board"), one place to work · shared band/position/drawer/shortlist · convergence becomes visible · kills the "why two similar screens?" confusion · fewer nav items.
Reasons to keep separate
Different rhythms — a briefing you check vs a tool you operate · different data freshness (6h cycle vs live) · the mode switch adds one cognitive step · power users may want each at a fixed URL.

My take: merge them. The lens switch is a smaller tax than maintaining two near-identical boards, and convergence is a real new signal.
