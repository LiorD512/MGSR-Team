↑ Fully interactive — tap tabs, the AI scout, filters & the “More” sheet

What's improved for mobile

👍
Thumb-first navigation. A 5-slot bottom bar with a raised AI-Scout action button puts the top tasks within thumb reach, replacing the buried hamburger menu.
🗂️
Bottom sheets, not sidebars. Secondary tools (Market Radar, Releases, Contacts…) live in a swipe-up “More” sheet instead of a cramped drawer.
📊
Swipeable KPI & stat strips. Dense desktop grids become horizontally scrollable cards that stay legible on a narrow screen.
🎛️
Native-feel filters. A dedicated filter sheet with sliders and chip toggles replaces tiny desktop dropdowns.
🌐
Platform themes + RTL. Men / Women / Youth palettes and full Hebrew right-to-left layout, all preserved from the production design system.
✋
44px+ touch targets. Every tap target respects mobile accessibility sizing with active-state feedback.
